37 Dynamical systems and ergodic theory

Dynamical systems

If you apply the same rule over and over, when is the long-run behavior predictable and when chaotic?

A dynamical system is a space of states together with a rule for how states evolve, either in discrete steps by iterating a map or continuously along a flow. The area studies long-term behavior: fixed and periodic points, attractors, stability, and sensitive dependence on initial conditions, popularly called chaos. Ergodic theory studies the same systems statistically, through invariant measures and time averages.

The subject classifies systems up to conjugacy, a change of coordinates that matches orbits, and looks for invariants such as entropy, rotation numbers and Lyapunov exponents. It draws on topology, measure theory, differential geometry and complex analysis, and it grew out of celestial mechanics. Ergodic methods have in turn solved problems in number theory and combinatorics, for example on arithmetic progressions and Diophantine approximation.

The doubling map

The doubling map sends a number xxx in [0,1)[0,1)[0,1) to 2x2x2x minus its integer part. In binary it deletes the first digit: 0.1011…0.1011\ldots0.1011… goes to 0.011…0.011\ldots0.011…. The point 13=0.0101…\tfrac13=0.0101\ldots31​=0.0101… has period 2, since 13→23→13\tfrac13\to\tfrac23\to\tfrac1331​→32​→31​. Two points that agree in their first 20 binary digits stay close for about 20 steps and then separate. Rational points are eventually periodic, while almost every point has a dense orbit.

Central objects

Orbit
The sequence or curve of states visited from a given starting state.
Invariant measure
A measure on the state space that is unchanged by the dynamics.
Ergodicity
The property that every invariant set has measure zero or full measure.
Entropy
A number measuring the exponential growth rate of distinguishable orbit segments.
Lyapunov exponent
The exponential rate at which nearby orbits separate or approach.
Attractor
A closed invariant set that nearby orbits approach as time goes to infinity.
Conjugacy
An invertible change of coordinates carrying one system to another.

Landmark theorems

  1. Poincaré recurrence theorem1890

    Let TTT be a measure-preserving map of a finite measure space and AAA a measurable set. Then almost every point of AAA returns to AAA infinitely often under iteration of TTT.

  2. Birkhoff ergodic theorem1931

    Let TTT preserve a probability measure μ\muμ and let fff be integrable. Then the averages 1n∑k=0n−1f(Tkx)\frac1n\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}f(T^kx)n1​∑k=0n−1​f(Tkx) converge for almost every xxx. If TTT is ergodic, the limit is ∫f dμ\int f\,d\mu∫fdμ.

  3. Denjoy's theorem1932

    An orientation-preserving C2C^2C2 diffeomorphism of the circle with irrational rotation number α\alphaα is topologically conjugate to the rotation by α\alphaα.

  4. Kolmogorov-Arnold-Moser theorem

    Take an analytic integrable Hamiltonian system satisfying a nondegeneracy condition and perturb it by a sufficiently small smooth Hamiltonian term. Then the invariant tori whose frequencies satisfy a Diophantine condition survive, slightly deformed. They fill a set of positive measure whose complement has measure tending to zero with the size of the perturbation.

  5. Hartman-Grobman theorem

    Let ppp be a fixed point of a C1C^1C1 diffeomorphism fff of Rn\mathbb{R}^nRn such that no eigenvalue of Df(p)Df(p)Df(p) has modulus 1. Then near ppp the map fff is topologically conjugate to the linear map Df(p)Df(p)Df(p) near 000.

Open problems and frontiers

  • Local connectivity of the Mandelbrot set

    Is the Mandelbrot set locally connected? A positive answer would imply that hyperbolic maps are dense among complex quadratic polynomials.

  • Entropy of the standard map

    Does the Chirikov standard map of the torus have positive metric entropy with respect to area for some parameter value?

  • Furstenberg's times 2, times 3 conjecture

    Is Lebesgue measure the only non-atomic ergodic Borel probability measure on the circle invariant under both x↦2xx\mapsto2xx↦2x and x↦3xx\mapsto3xx↦3x modulo 1?
