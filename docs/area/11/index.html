Number theory

Which whole numbers are sums of two squares, and why do primes thin out the way they do?

Number theory studies the whole numbers and the questions that grow out of them: how the primes are distributed, which equations have solutions in integers or in fractions, and how well irrational numbers can be approximated by fractions. Its problems are often easy to state and hard to solve, and some stood for centuries before a proof was found.

The methods come from all over mathematics. Analytic number theory uses complex analysis, through the zeta function and its relatives, to count primes. Algebraic number theory enlarges the integers to rings of algebraic numbers. Arithmetic geometry treats equations as curves and higher-dimensional shapes. Modular forms and the Langlands program tie these strands together, and computation with large primes underlies modern cryptography.

Sums of two squares

Try to write primes as a sum of two squares: 5=1+45=1+45=1+4, 13=4+913=4+913=4+9, 17=1+1617=1+1617=1+16, 29=4+2529=4+2529=4+25, 37=1+3637=1+3637=1+36. But 333, 777, 111111, 191919 and 232323 cannot be written this way. The pattern: an odd prime is a sum of two squares exactly when it leaves remainder 1 on division by 4. One direction is easy, since a square leaves remainder 0 or 1. The other was stated by Fermat and first proved by Euler.

Multiplication modulo N has hidden structure

Points 0 to N-1 sit on a circle. Each point i is joined to the point m times i, modulo N. Change m and N, or press Play to sweep m through fractions.

What to notice
  • With m = 2 the lines trace a cardioid, and with m = 3 a nephroid. In general the envelope has m-1 cusps.
  • The gold dots are the fixed points, where m times i equals i modulo N. For whole m there are exactly gcd(m-1, N) of them.
  • Between whole values of m the envelope blurs. The sharp curves belong to integer m.

Central objects

Prime number
A whole number above 1 divisible only by 1 and itself.
Congruence
Two integers are congruent modulo nnn when their difference is divisible by nnn.
Diophantine equation
A polynomial equation for which integer or rational solutions are sought.
Zeta and L-functions
Functions of a complex variable that encode primes and other arithmetic data.
Number field
A field obtained from the rationals by adjoining a root of a polynomial with rational coefficients.
Elliptic curve
A smooth cubic curve such as y2=x3+ax+by^2=x^3+ax+by2=x3+ax+b, whose points form an abelian group.
Modular form
A highly symmetric analytic function on the upper half-plane whose coefficients carry arithmetic information.
