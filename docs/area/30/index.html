30 Functions of a complex variable

Complex analysis

Why is a function with a complex derivative fixed everywhere by its values on one small circle?

Complex analysis studies functions of one complex variable that have a derivative in the complex sense. Such functions are called holomorphic. The condition looks mild but is very strong: a holomorphic function is infinitely differentiable, equals its Taylor series near every point, and is fixed everywhere on a connected domain by its values on any small disk. The area also covers meromorphic functions, conformal maps and Riemann surfaces.

The main tools are contour integrals, power series and the geometry of conformal maps, which preserve angles. Results often trade analysis for geometry or topology: integrals are computed by locating poles, and zeros are counted by winding numbers. The subject feeds number theory through zeta and modular functions, and supplies methods to potential theory, fluid flow, differential equations and the theory of algebraic curves.

Analytic maps keep right angles

A square grid in the complex plane is carried to w = (1 − t)z + t f(z). Teal lines were horizontal, red lines were vertical; the faint grid is where they started.

What to notice
  • Wherever the derivative is not zero, a teal line and a red line still cross at 90 degrees, however much the squares are bent and stretched. This is what conformal means.
  • Each little square stays nearly a square: it is rotated and scaled, not sheared. Drag the dot and read the angle.
  • At a critical point (gold ring) the derivative is zero and the angle doubles, so z² turns the corner at 0 into a straight line. At a pole such as 0 for 1/z, the grid is clipped.

Central objects

Holomorphic function
A complex-valued function on an open set of the plane that is complex differentiable at every point.
Meromorphic function
A function that is holomorphic except at isolated poles.
Residue
The coefficient of 1/(z−a)1/(z-a)1/(z−a) in the Laurent expansion of a function around an isolated singularity aaa.
Conformal map
A holomorphic map with nowhere vanishing derivative; it preserves angles and orientation.
Analytic continuation
Extending a holomorphic function to a larger domain; the extension is unique when it exists.
Riemann surface
A connected one-dimensional complex manifold, the natural home of multivalued functions such as the square root.
Entire function
A function holomorphic on the whole complex plane.

  3. Liouville's theorem

    Every bounded entire function is constant.

  4. Riemann mapping theorem1851

    Every nonempty simply connected open subset of C\mathbb{C}C other than C\mathbb{C}C itself is biholomorphic to the open unit disk. Riemann stated the theorem in 1851; complete proofs came later.

  5. Picard's little theorem1879

    A nonconstant entire function takes every complex value with at most one exception.

  6. Uniformization theorem1907

    Every simply connected Riemann surface is biholomorphic to exactly one of the Riemann sphere, the complex plane and the open unit disk.

Open problems and frontiers

  • Bloch's constant

    Every holomorphic fff on the unit disk with f′(0)=1f'(0)=1f′(0)=1 maps some subdomain bijectively onto a disk of a universal radius BBB; the best value of BBB is unknown.

  • Sendov's conjecture

    If all zeros of a polynomial of degree at least 2 lie in the closed unit disk, is every zero within distance 1 of a critical point? This is known for all sufficiently high degrees.
