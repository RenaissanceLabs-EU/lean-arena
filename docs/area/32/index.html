32 Several complex variables and analytic spaces

Sev. complex variables

Why can a holomorphic function of two complex variables never have an isolated singularity?

This area studies holomorphic functions of two or more complex variables and the spaces on which they live: domains in Cn\mathbb{C}^nCn, complex manifolds, and analytic spaces, which are allowed to have singular points. New phenomena appear that have no counterpart in one variable. Some domains force every holomorphic function on them to extend to a larger domain, and no analog of the Riemann mapping theorem holds.

Methods mix analysis, geometry and algebra. Plurisubharmonic functions and the inhomogeneous Cauchy-Riemann equations ∂ˉu=f\bar\partial u=f∂ˉu=f, solved with L2L^2L2 estimates, carry the analytic side. Sheaves and their cohomology organize the passage from local to global, a language that was largely developed here. The subject overlaps with algebraic geometry over the complex numbers, with differential geometry through Kähler metrics, and with partial differential equations.

Central objects

Holomorphic function of several variables
A continuous function on an open set of complex n-space that is holomorphic in each variable separately.
Domain of holomorphy
A domain carrying a holomorphic function that cannot be extended holomorphically across any boundary point.
Plurisubharmonic function
An upper semicontinuous function whose restriction to every complex line is subharmonic.
Pseudoconvex domain
A domain in Cn\mathbb{C}^nCn with a continuous plurisubharmonic exhaustion function.
Stein manifold
A complex manifold that can be embedded as a closed complex submanifold of some CN\mathbb{C}^NCN.
Analytic set
A set that is locally the common zero set of finitely many holomorphic functions.
Coherent analytic sheaf
A sheaf of modules locally presented by finitely many generators and relations over the holomorphic functions.

Open problems and frontiers

  • Boundary regularity of biholomorphic maps

    Does every biholomorphic map between bounded domains in Cn\mathbb{C}^nCn with smooth boundary extend smoothly to the boundaries? This is known for strictly pseudoconvex domains.

  • Green-Griffiths-Lang conjecture

    On a complex projective variety of general type, do all nonconstant entire holomorphic curves lie in one proper algebraic subvariety?
