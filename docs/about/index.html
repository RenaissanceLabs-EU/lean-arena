About

A map, and a place to practice

Renaissance Labs is the lab for frontier labs. We build the environments, evaluations and data that bring intelligence to every field of human work, and we give them away. This site is the mathematics part of that work.

The idea

Mathematics is the one field where an answer can be checked by machine with no judgment involved. That makes it the best place to learn how intelligence improves itself. This site has two halves that feed each other: a map of all of mathematics with its formalization status, and Lean environments that turn the gaps on the map into problems a model can practice on. As models get better at proving, they can formalize more of the map.

How the map is made

  • Structure. The 63 areas and 534 subareas are the top two levels of the Mathematics Subject Classification 2020, the scheme used by zbMATH Open and Mathematical Reviews. We use it unchanged, and group the areas into eight families for the map.
  • Area pages. Written for this site and checked for form by a script. Mathematical statements were written from memory by a language model and have not all been reviewed by specialists; years follow one convention noted on each page where it is ambiguous. Spot an error and tell us.
  • Coverage. Counted from the source of Mathlib at commit 5e932f97dd: every theorem and lemma declaration, filed under MSC areas by a hand-written mapping of Mathlib directories (tools/mathlib_coverage.py). A directory that covers several areas is split evenly. This is a measure of size, not of depth, and the mapping is ours: a few areas show almost nothing because Mathlib has no directory for them, even though a result or two may exist elsewhere.
  • Frontier lists. Read directly from Mathlib's own tracking files.

Interactive figures

Some area pages carry a three.js figure you can drag and change. We add one only when it shows something prose cannot: how a matrix reshapes space, why a Lorenz trajectory cannot be predicted, what a Möbius strip does to an arrow. Most areas have none, on purpose; a figure that decorates is worse than none. Each one is computed from the mathematics, not animated by hand, and each caption states only what the figure shows. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

Add an environment

An environment is a Python generator that returns a Lean task: a statement with sorry, plus a reference proof. The contract is in AUTHORING.md in the repository. Good environments are true, varied, need a real idea at higher difficulty, and are not closed by a single automation call. Open a pull request.
